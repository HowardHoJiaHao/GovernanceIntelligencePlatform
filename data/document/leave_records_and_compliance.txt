=== LEAVE RECORDS AND COMPLIANCE POLICY ===

Section 5.12: Record Keeping

- All leave applications, approvals, and rejections are recorded in the HR Portal.
- Leave records must be retained for seven years, in line with the Document Retention rules in the Corporate Governance Framework.
- HR sends a monthly leave balance report to all Department Heads.

Section 5.13: Unauthorised Absence

- Absence without approved leave is recorded as unpaid leave.
- Absence without approval or a valid reason for more than 2 consecutive working days is treated as a breach of the employment contract and referred to HR for disciplinary action.
- Employees who submit 3 or more late leave applications within 6 months are reported to their Department Head.

Section 5.14: Roles and Responsibilities

- Employees: apply for leave on time and check their leave balance.
- Line Managers: approve or reject leave requests within 3 working days.
- Department Heads: ensure enough staff cover during holidays and blackout periods.
- HR Department: maintain the holiday calendar, leave balances, and this policy.
- Internal Audit: review the leave records of at least one department every quarter.

Section 5.15: Policy Review

- This policy is reviewed every year by the HR Director and approved by the Divisional Managing Director.
- Policy Owner: HR Department.
- Effective date: 1 January 2026.
