=== ANNUAL LEAVE POLICY ===

Section 5.5: Entitlement

Annual leave per calendar year depends on years of service:
- Less than 2 years: 14 days.
- 2 to 5 years: 18 days.
- More than 5 years: 22 days.
- Senior Managers and above: 24 days, regardless of years of service.
- New joiners receive leave pro-rated by completed months of service in their first year.

Section 5.6: Applying for Leave

- All leave must be applied for through the HR Portal.
- 1 to 3 days: apply at least 3 working days in advance; approved by the Line Manager.
- 4 to 10 days: apply at least 14 days in advance; approved by the Department Head.
- More than 10 consecutive days: apply at least 30 days in advance; approved by the HR Director.
- Emergency leave may be applied for on the same day, but a reason must be entered in the HR Portal within 2 working days.

Section 5.7: Carry Forward and Encashment

- Up to 5 unused days may be carried forward to the next year.
- Carried forward days must be used by 31 March, otherwise they are forfeited.
- Unused leave is not paid out, except on resignation or retirement, when it is paid at the ordinary daily rate.

Section 5.8: Blackout Periods

- Finance staff may not take annual leave during the first 10 working days of January (year-end closing).
- Procurement staff may not take more than 3 consecutive days of leave during the annual tender cycle in September.
