=== COMPANY HOLIDAY POLICY ===

Section 5.1: Public Holidays

- All employees are entitled to 14 paid public holidays per calendar year.
- 11 are gazetted public holidays, including the 5 compulsory holidays: National Day, Malaysia Day, Workers' Day, the Birthday of the Yang di-Pertuan Agong, and the Birthday of the State Ruler (or Federal Territory Day).
- 3 are additional company holidays: Company Foundation Day (15 March), Christmas Eve, and New Year's Eve.
- HR publishes the official holiday calendar for the next year by 30 November.

Section 5.2: Holidays Falling on a Rest Day

- If a public holiday falls on a Saturday or Sunday, the next working day is a replacement holiday.
- If two public holidays fall on the same day, the next working day is also a replacement holiday.

Section 5.3: Working on a Public Holiday

- Employees may only be asked to work on a public holiday with Department Head approval.
- Employees who work on a public holiday receive three times their ordinary daily rate, or one replacement day off taken within 60 days, at the employee's choice.
- Shift workers in Security and IT Operations follow the rotating holiday roster set by their Department Head.

Section 5.4: Swapping Holidays

- Employees may swap up to 2 non-compulsory public holidays per year for other religious or cultural festivals.
- Swap requests must be approved by HR at least 30 days in advance.
- The 5 compulsory holidays cannot be swapped.
